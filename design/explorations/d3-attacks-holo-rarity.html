Attacks, holo and rarity

Starter attack per job, quieter animated holos, rarity colours and the cursor slot.

Fighting: slash at changing angles, with sparks

Three automatic hits, then a short pause. Each slash appears, thins and fades in about a quarter of a second.

Mining: a heavy hit (shockwave, cracks, rock chips, dust)

Logging: the same slash, with wood chips

Holo finishes: animated, quieter, patterned

Colour only appears where the moving light crosses the pattern; the rest stays silver.
